# Game brief

An isometric action RPG (camera and controls like Path of Exile 2 / Diablo IV).

## World
Set in an "It Takes Two" style world: the heroes are toy-sized, and the levels
are ordinary places blown up to giant scale and made playful and magical: a
backyard, a kid's bedroom, a garage band's practice room, a school talent show
stage. Everyday objects are terrain, enemies and hazards (amp cables, wind-up
toys, metronomes, lawn gnomes, vacuum cleaners). Tone: bright, funny, cartoon
physics.

## Weapon
A second class: the player's weapon is an electric guitar (with amp, pedals,
pick and cable as part of the kit); rock music and guitar playing are the class
fantasy. It can be swung like a club, played to fire sound, and used to cast.

## The task
Design 16 skills for this class: 4 melee (the guitar as a weapon), 4 ranged
(sound and objects fired at range), 4 caster (spells made from music: chords,
effects pedals, feedback, harmonics) and 4 utility (one movement, one defence,
one utility, one ultimate). They must be:
- real ARPG skills that make the play loop fun (clearing packs, single-target
  bosses, movement, defence), like POE2 or Diablo;
- not too complicated: one clear idea per skill, readable at a glance;
- built with real scaling potential (tags, stats, how they grow into a build
  and combo with each other);
- authentic to guitar (real techniques, gear, music theory, rock culture) and
  to the toy-world setting;
- described with the animation and feel (wind-up, impact, hit-stop, VFX, sound).
