Read BRIEF.md, INSPIRATION.md, RESEARCH.md and ASSOCIATIONS.md (and CURRENT_SKILLS.md if it exists, as the kit to improve on). All research and associations are in those files.

Design the skills from BRIEF.md. Skill N must fuse pair N of INSPIRATION.md, in its fixed role: (1) the real research paper's finding, (2) the real skill or mechanic from that other-genre game, and (3) a theme idea, all condensed into ONE very simple POE2-style skill. The paper's mechanism and the game's skill must shape the RULE of the skill, not just its name.

Keep the ARPG core: readable at a glance, one idea per skill, a clear generator/spender economy, coverage of pack clear, single target, movement and defence, and real scaling through tags/stats. Give the class 2-3 small core systems shared by the skills (like a resource and a timing mechanic). Each role group should feel distinct while combining with the others. Never use the same theme idea for two skills.

Write SKILLS.md. Start with the core systems and a coverage table. For each skill: name; role; tags; what it does (max 3 sentences); sources (paper: title, authors, year; game and its skill; theme idea) and how they were condensed into one idea; animation and feel; scaling; synergies. End with 3 example builds.
