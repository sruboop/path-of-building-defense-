- Deflect window shrinks if you mash [P1] <-> Barrel window fixed regardless of attack speed [CS]: both are anti-mash timing rules.
- Deflect spark/clang [P1] <-> hit-stop and CRACK on Barrel [CS]: same instant-confirmation feel for a timed success.
- Attack = defence in Sekiro [P1] <-> Check Swing counter-hit [CS]: both reward a precise cancel into a counter.
- Deflect [P1] <-> foul tip off the bat / catcher's glove [BB]: tipping a fast pitch away is a deflect.
