# Game brief

An isometric action RPG (camera and controls like Path of Exile 2 / Diablo IV).

## World
Set in an "It Takes Two" style world: the heroes are toy-sized, and the levels
are ordinary places blown up to giant scale and made playful and magical: a
backyard, a kid's bedroom, a garage, a little-league baseball field at dusk.
Everyday objects are terrain, enemies and hazards (sprinklers, wind-up toys,
lawn gnomes, vacuum cleaners). Tone: bright, funny, cartoon physics.

## Weapon
The player's weapon is a baseball bat; baseball is the class fantasy.

## The task
Design 16 skills for this weapon/class. They must be:
- real ARPG skills that make the moment-to-moment play loop fun (clearing
  packs, single-target bosses, movement, defence), like POE2 or Diablo;
- not too complicated: one clear idea per skill, readable in a glance;
- built with real scaling potential (what stats/tags scale them, how they
  grow into a build, how they combo/synergise with each other);
- authentic to baseball (real techniques, rules, situations, terminology)
  and to the toy-world setting;
- described with the animation/feel (wind-up, impact, hit-stop, VFX, sound).
